Open decisions

  1. Duplicate customers. Some companies appear more than once in Sage, under the same or a similar name, or matched to the same Salesforce account. Proposed: load one customer per company, the one invoiced most recently (if none was invoiced, the one with more complete data). The others are not loaded; their Sage IDs are kept on the surviving customer so Sage history can be traced. Exceptions, kept as separate customers: records that were both invoiced from different countries, and records of a company that has several Subnet accounts (see next item). See Duplicate Action, groups to merge.
  2. L3Harris Subnet IDs. L3Harris has three Sage records and five Subnet accounts in Salesforce (695, 716, 720, 784, 1026). Proposed by date: C-00333 L3 Technologies (last invoiced Apr 2024) to 695, C-00385 (Apr 2025) to 784, C-00562 (Jul 2026) to 1026. Subnet accounts 716 and 720 were created alongside 695 (their PO numbers run in sequence with 695's) and have no Sage record of their own. Confirm the three, and say whether 716 and 720 become their own customers. Marked "Subnet ID to assign" in Question Categories.

Notes

  1. Customer ID. Sage IDs are not consistent. Customers with a Sage ID in the C-##### format keep it. The customers with an ID in another format () get the next numbers after the highest Sage C-##### (): . Their original Sage ID stays in Sage Customer ID.
  2. External ID. Same value as Customer ID, so later loads and updates can find each customer by it.
  3. New customers. After the load, NetSuite should auto-generate customer numbers in the same C-##### format, starting at , the number after the last one in the load. In NetSuite: Setup > Company > Auto-Generated Numbers, Entities subtab, Customer: prefix C-, minimum 5 digits, initial number . Allow Override must be on during the import so the loaded numbers are kept.
  4. Investor records, open question. Sage has active records numbered C00001 to C00016 that look like investors and note holders (for example General Catalyst, Index Ventures, Interest Accrued on Notes), not customers. Should they be loaded into NetSuite as customers? They are in this file, marked "Investor" in Question Categories and listed first. If they are left out, no renumbering is needed and new numbers start right after .
  5. Customer Category, open question. A list MinIO defines in NetSuite to group customers for reporting; NetSuite does not ship with values. The four values in the template (Direct Customer, Reseller, End Customer, OEM/MSP) are set from the Sage customer type: Direct Sales to Direct Customer, Direct Sales with Reseller to End Customer, Reseller to Reseller. These Sage types have no obvious category and are left blank, marked "SOA Category Unknown" in Question Categories and listed after the Salesforce matches: .
  6. Status. Every customer in this load is active in Sage (not marked inactive), so every customer is set to CUSTOMER-Closed Won. Customers that are renewals can be changed to CUSTOMER-Renewal when deal data is loaded.
  7. Customer Invoice Email. The address invoices go to. Where Sage has an accounts payable or purchasing address (for example ap@, accounts.payable@, invoices@, purchasing@), that address is used; otherwise the first address Sage has. Every other address for the customer goes to Additional Emails.
  8. Reseller billing. For customers Sage invoices through a reseller, the reseller is spelled out in Billed To (Reseller), Reseller Address and Reseller Invoice Email, with Salesforce partners alongside, so it can be set up to best practice.
  9. Subsidiary. The Sage exports do not record an entity for any customer. All customers are loaded to MinIO, Inc., the parent subsidiary, and bill from it.
  10. Terms. Sage terms are mapped to the NetSuite list: Due on Receipt, Net 10, Net 15, Net 30, Net 45, Net 60, Net 150. Sage "30 Days" becomes Net 30, and so on. Sage day counts with no match on the list become new terms of the same length, which must be created in NetSuite before the load: . Sage "5 years" becomes Net 1825 (5 x 365 days). "Due per Contract" is loaded as Sage has it and needs research before the load: . customers have no terms in Sage and are left blank.
  11. Parent Company. Salesforce links some accounts to a parent company, for example Royal Caribbean International to Royal Caribbean Group. For customers the parent is not a Sage customer: MinIO has no billing history with it and deals only with the subsidiary financially. Those parents are not loaded, and Parent Company is left blank for those customers. Parent links are kept where the parent is itself a customer in this load.
  12. Salesforce matching. Customers are matched to Salesforce accounts by name. 37 names were close but not identical; each was reviewed on 2026-10-26 against city, country and email domain. 35 were confirmed. Two were left unmatched as different companies: Ministry of Defence (Singapore, not the UK ministry) and Bae Systems (kept separate from Salesforce's BAE Systems US).
  13. Sage data corrections. Corrected in the load only; Sage itself is unchanged. Proctor & Gamble: the billing address is in Lodz, Poland (street, postal code and VAT number are Polish), so the city "Cincinnati" is corrected to Lodz. Orange Côte d'Ivoire: country France corrected to Cote d'Ivoire, city Abidjan. NSN Brasil: the Sage city field held an attention note; city corrected to Sao Jose dos Pinhais, postal code 83085-058, and the note moved to Attention. Three addresses with garbled accents (for example "MontrÃ©al") are repaired to the correct characters. Five US postal codes that lost their leading zero in Sage (for example 7936) are restored to five digits (07936).
  14. Address parsing. Sage prints each address as text, so it was split into fields for the load. "Attn" and contact names in the Sage address go to Attention. VAT, RFC and GSTIN numbers written into the Sage street line go to VAT ID when Sage's Tax ID field is empty. Phone extensions are written as " x123" to fit NetSuite's 21-character phone field. Every value in the load was checked back against the raw Sage and Salesforce exports with no differences left.
  15. Subnet ID. Subnet IDs are a key identifier: each Subnet account is its own NetSuite customer, and customers with different Subnet IDs are never merged. Salesforce holds one account per Subnet account, so a company with several Subnet accounts (for example L3Harris, with five) appears in Salesforce several times. Where a company has several Sage records and several Subnet accounts, they are paired by date: the earlier Sage record with the earlier Subnet account (Applied Materials C-00103 to 348 and C-00462 to 879, Davidson C-00371 to 755 and C-00592 to 1067, Saudi Telecom C-00241 to 537 and C-00542 to 998). Where Salesforce has a Subnet account with no matching Sage customer, it is not in this load.
  16. Added columns. Question Categories, Sage Customer ID, Salesforce Account ID, Additional Emails and the reseller columns are not standard template columns. Sage Customer ID, Salesforce Account ID and Subnet ID are kept as their own fields so the source of each customer is explicit.
